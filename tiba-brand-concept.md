# Tiba: Brand Concept (The Friendly Colleague)

## Mission
Give solo clinicians one simple, friendly place to book visits, write and sign notes, and find them again, so paperwork never gets in the way of care.

## Primary audience
A solo clinician (such as a therapist) running their own small practice with no staff. They are often newly independent and not very technical, they juggle Word, fillable PDFs, and Google Forms, and they find most clinic software intimidating.

## Positioning against competitors
Tiba sits between the two options a solo clinician has today.

- **The patchwork (Word, PDF forms, Google Forms):** free and familiar, but notes end up scattered and hard to find. Tiba is just as easy to start with, and keeps everything in one place.
- **Big clinic and hospital systems:** powerful, but built for teams with admin staff, and they take training to use. Tiba is built for one person and needs no training.

In one sentence: for the solo clinician who finds clinic software intimidating, Tiba is the note-keeping tool that feels like a helpful colleague, not a system to be trained on.

## Three brand pillars
1. **Simple first.** Three screens, plain words, nothing to learn. If a feature needs a manual, it doesn't belong.
2. **On your side.** Tiba encourages and never scolds. When something goes wrong, it says what to do next.
3. **Safe hands.** Warm does not mean careless. Notes are signed, saved, private, and easy to find.

## Voice and tone
Tiba talks like a colleague across the desk: plain, warm, and brief. It says "you", keeps sentences short, and marks small wins lightly ("Nice work. Amina's note is signed and saved."). It is friendly but never jokes about health or privacy.

- **Words we always use:** you, let's, simple, done, safe
- **Words we never use:** platform, solution, seamless, optimise, invalid

## Visual feel
Tiba looks light, warm, and open, like a tidy consulting room with the morning sun coming in, not a hospital corridor. The palette is Fern & Lilac: a soft off-white background (#FBFAFD), pale green panels, a deep fern green (#2D6A4F) for headings and buttons, near-black text (#25282D), and small touches of lilac (#CDBDF4) as the accent. Corners are generously rounded, spacing is roomy, and headlines are set in the warm serif Young Serif above plain, easy-to-read body text in Figtree. Buttons are large and clearly labelled, icons are simple line drawings, and there are no stock photos of stethoscopes or hospital wards. Everything feels just as comfortable on a phone as on a computer.
